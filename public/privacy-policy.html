PROPERTY PROS PSL LLC

Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

This policy explains how Property Pros PSL LLC handles personal information collected through our website, lead forms, customer communications, and service operations, including pool and lawn service inquiries.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide, such as your name, telephone number, email address, service address, service interests, appointment preferences, and messages. We also maintain customer service records and records of text-message permission and opt-out requests. When you submit a Facebook lead form, we receive the information you submit and identifying information about the form or advertisement.

How we use information

We use this information to respond to inquiries, provide estimates, schedule and perform services, communicate about appointments and upcoming services, support customers, and maintain business records. We send marketing and sales-follow-up texts only with your permission.

Text-message privacy

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-message opt-in data and consent will not be sold or shared with third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes. Service providers may process information on our behalf as needed to deliver messages, manage customer records, or provide requested services; this does not authorize them to use your mobile information for their own marketing.

Other disclosures

We may provide necessary information to personnel and service providers supporting our business, and disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, or prevent fraud. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

Text-message consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase. Reply STOP to unsubscribe from our texts or HELP for assistance. You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. We may retain records needed for outstanding services, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and honoring opt-out requests.

Retention and security

We retain information as needed for the purposes described above and applicable recordkeeping obligations. No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Third-party services

Facebook and other external services have their own privacy policies. This policy covers our handling of information after we receive it.

Policy updates and contact

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. For privacy questions or requests, email jody.g@propprospsl.com.
